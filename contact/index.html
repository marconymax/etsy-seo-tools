Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to sign up or create an account?

No. Every tool on Etsy SEO Tools is freely accessible directly in your web browser without registration, passwords, or shop authorization.

Will this tool ever charge a subscription?

No. Our core tools are designed to remain 100% free for independent Etsy sellers. Because the code runs entirely on the client side without costly backend server infrastructure, we can sustain the site at zero marginal cost.

Does this tool store my listing drafts or shop data?

Never. When you paste your product title and description into the generator, the algorithm processes the text locally on your computer or phone. Nothing is transmitted to our servers or stored in any database.

Can I suggest an additional feature or algorithm improvement?

Absolutely! Send us an email at the address above. We actively update our stop-word lists and intent boosters based on feedback from real Etsy shop owners.
