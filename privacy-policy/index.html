Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026. This policy explains how Etsy SEO Tools operates as a client-side static website, handles cookies, and protects your information.

1. Overview and Core Philosophy

At Etsy SEO Tools ("we," "our," or "us"), accessible via our website, protecting the privacy and creative property of independent artisans and visitors is fundamental to our mission.

Our application operates as a static website. We do not require users to create accounts, log in, or provide personal details such as names, passwords, credit card numbers, or Etsy API tokens.

2. Local Client-Side Processing of Listing Data

When you use our tools (including the Etsy Tag & Keyword Generator) by pasting listing titles, product descriptions, or keywords into our inputs:

  • All textual analysis, n-gram tokenization, and tag generation algorithms execute entirely inside your local web browser using client-side JavaScript.
  • Your listing text, product drafts, and generated tags are never transmitted to an external server or remote database.
  • We do not record, store, or monitor your proprietary product concepts, keywords, or pricing information.

3. Server Logs and Web Hosting

Our static site files are hosted on high-performance static hosting infrastructure (such as Vercel). Like standard web hosts, the underlying hosting platform may automatically record standard web server request logs, including:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) addresses (anonymized where applicable)
  • Browser type and version
  • Referring and exit pages
  • Date and time stamps of page requests

These logs are used solely for infrastructure security, DDOS prevention, and server reliability monitoring. They are not linked to any personally identifiable information.

4. Cookies, Web Analytics, and Advertising Disclosure

To maintain this website as a 100% free resource for makers without charging subscriptions, we may implement third-party analytics and advertising networks:

Google AdSense and DoubleClick Cookies

Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on this website. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.

  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendor use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

Web Analytics

We may utilize privacy-friendly analytics tools (such as Google Analytics or Vercel Web Analytics) to understand aggregate metrics, such as overall visitor counts, popular pages, and device types. These services may place first-party or third-party cookies on your browser to measure interaction metrics in aggregate.

5. External Links

Our site may contain links to third-party websites (e.g., Etsy, Inc., external documentation, or educational articles). We have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content, privacy policies, or practices of any third-party sites or services.

6. Children's Information

Our tools and educational guides are directed at business owners, independent makers, and adult creators. We do not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect modifications to our site features, legal requirements, or advertising partners. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
